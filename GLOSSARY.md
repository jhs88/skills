# Glossary

- **Published skill**: an entry explicitly listed in Matt Pocock's plugin manifest, in an allowed engineering or productivity path.
- **Managed subtree**: copied skill content under `skills/mattpocock/`; synchronization owns its additions, updates, and removals.
- **Local skill**: future owner-authored content under `skills/local/`, never reconciled from upstream.
- **Provenance lock**: `upstreams.lock.json`, identifying the commit, manifest, skill selection, and copied content hashes.
- **Installation**: configuring an agent runtime to load selected skills. This repository's sync does not perform installation.
